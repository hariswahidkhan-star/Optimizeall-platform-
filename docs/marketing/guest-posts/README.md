## Strategy

- **Give first.** Publishers accept guest posts that teach their readers something useful. Every article is
  vendor-neutral, has original frameworks or worked examples, and makes sense with the partner link removed.
- **One partner mention, one link.** Each article contains at most one natural, contextual brand mention/link to the
  partner (plus the relationship disclosure in the bio). Never add more links after acceptance.
- **Match the audience.** Pick the article whose angle fits the publication; adapt examples, spelling and length to the
  publication's style guide. Never submit the same article to two publications — each placement gets an exclusive
  version (rewrite at least the introduction, examples and headings for any second use, or write a new piece).
- **Build authority, not links.** The goal is reach, brand mentions, topical authority and referral traffic (see the
  strategy doc), not PageRank.

## Outreach process

1. **Prospect.** Build a list of 10–20 publications per audience (project management, finance/accounting, nursing
   education, careers, AI/edtech, IT governance). Qualify: real editorial team, relevant audience, published guest
   guidelines, no "pay for a follow link" offers, recent activity.
2. **Research.** Read three recent articles; note one specific point to reference in the pitch; check guidelines for
   length, format, bio rules and link policy.
3. **Pitch.** Use the file's email template; personalise the first two lines; offer one angle (keep the other two for a
   follow-up). Send from a named person at Optimize All.
4. **Follow up once** after 7–10 days with a second angle. Stop after two unanswered emails.
5. **Draft.** Adapt the article to the publication's style; keep the partner link optional and clearly disclosed.
6. **Edit and approve.** Accept the editor's changes; if they remove the partner link, accept that too — the article
   still earns a brand mention in the bio and reach for Optimize All.
7. **Publish and verify.** Check the live page: link attributes (`rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`), disclosure in the
   bio, correct anchor and URL.
8. **Promote.** Share on Optimize All's channels (with UTM-tagged links to the publication), thank the editor, and log
   results.
9. **Report.** Monthly: placements, referral clicks, brand mentions, and relationships worth a second piece.

## Compliance notes

- **Link attributes.** Optimize All is the official marketing partner of PCI AI and Certuvo. Under Google's guidance on
  links that are part of advertising, sponsorship or other compensation arrangements, the partner link in a guest post
  must be qualified with `rel="sponsored"` (or `rel="nofollow"`). Ask the editor to apply it and verify it on the live
  page. If a publication refuses to qualify the link, remove the link and keep a plain-text mention.
- **Disclosure.** Every author bio states: "Optimize All is the official marketing partner of PCI AI and Certuvo." This
  disclosure is required and does not count as the article's one brand mention. Follow applicable advertising and
  endorsement rules (for example, the FTC's Endorsement Guides in the US and the ASA/CAP rules in the UK).
- **No payment for links.** Never pay, or offer anything of value, to a publication in exchange for a followed link or
  for publishing a guest post. If a publication charges for sponsored content, treat it as advertising: it must be
  labelled as sponsored by the publisher and the link must be `rel="sponsored"`.
- **Facts.** Use only the partner facts in the strategy doc. No invented features, statistics, prices, customers or
  dates; no competitor names or comparisons; no time-limited discount codes. Refer readers to the official bodies
  (AICPA/NASBA, IMA, IIA, ISACA, CFA Institute, PMI, NCSBN) for exam requirements.
- **Clinical content.** The nursing-education article is educational, not clinical guidance; invite the publication's
  clinical reviewers to check it.
- **Anchor text.** Use descriptive, natural anchors (suggested in each file). Avoid exact-match keyword anchors repeated
  across placements.
